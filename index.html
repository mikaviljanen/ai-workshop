Build in ten-minute loops

First, split the roles

Driver. The facilitator types and runs the tool.
Owner. It's their task, so they decide what's good enough.
Tester. Makes fake inputs and tries to break the tool.
Sceptic. Asks every 20 minutes: would anyone use this, and how would we know it failed?

Then loop

  1. Smallest useful version first. Only the step that hurts most.
  2. Test at once on a fake input the tester has ready.
  3. Say what went wrong, not how to fix it.
  4. One change at a time, then rerun the same test.
  5. Write down what failed. Failures are half the demo.
Stuck 15 minutes? Wave at the floating facilitator.
Going in circles? New chat: the brief plus three lines on what you learned.
